MANIFEST 369

Privacy Policy

Your ritual is personal. We designed Manifest 369 with privacy in mind.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Overview

Manifest 369 - Affirmations ("Manifest 369", "the App") is provided by EEB Consultancy LLC ("we", "us", or "our").

Manifest 369 is designed as a local-first application. We do not operate user accounts or a server that receives your manifestation, intention, ritual, or reflection data.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To provide the App's features, Manifest 369 stores certain information locally on your device. Depending on how you use the App, this may include:

  • Your selected or custom-written intentions and affirmations.
  • Your active and previous intentions.
  • Your 369 ritual progress and completion history.
  • Morning, Afternoon, and Evening repetition counts.
  • Optional ritual reflections.
  • Journey length, progress, and completion information.
  • Streak and ritual activity information.
  • Your selected app language and accessibility preferences.
  • Reminder settings and reminder times.
  • Information necessary to remember onboarding and in-app offer states.

Custom intention text may contain personal or sensitive information if you choose to enter it. You control what you write in the App.

3. Morning Writing

During the Morning ritual, the App may ask you to write your intention multiple times. The text entered for these repetitions is used locally to validate the ritual and is not separately saved as a history of what you typed. The App stores the resulting ritual progress rather than each Morning entry.

4. Notifications

If you enable reminders, Manifest 369 requests permission to send local notifications. Notification scheduling and delivery are handled using Apple's notification system on your device.

Manifest 369 does not use a remote push-notification server. Your custom intention text is not placed in notification content.

You can disable notifications at any time through the App's reminder settings or your device's system settings.

5. Widgets

Manifest 369 may provide Home Screen widgets that display limited ritual status information such as progress, streak, Journey status, and visual state.

Widget data is stored locally in an Apple App Group shared between Manifest 369 and its widget extension. Your intention text and optional reflections are not included in the widget data.

6. Purchases and Subscriptions

Manifest 369 may offer Pro features through auto-renewable subscriptions and one-time in-app purchases.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not directly collect or store your payment card information, Apple Account credentials, or App Store payment details.

The App may receive information from StoreKit necessary to determine whether a purchase or subscription is valid and whether Pro access should be available.

Eligibility for introductory offers, including free trials, is determined through Apple's StoreKit services.

7. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Manifest 369 does not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs.

We do not use the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), and the App does not track your activity across apps or websites for advertising purposes.

8. Third-Party Services

The App relies on Apple-provided services where necessary for core platform functionality, including:

  • Apple App Store and StoreKit for purchases and subscriptions.
  • Apple system notifications for local reminders.
  • Apple system functionality for Home Screen widgets.
  • Apple's App Store review system when you choose to respond to a review request.

Your use of Apple services is also subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices.

9. iCloud and Device Backups

Manifest 369 does not currently use CloudKit or provide its own cross-device cloud synchronization service.

Data stored locally by the App may, however, be included in iCloud or computer backups of your device if you have enabled Apple's device backup features. Those backups are managed by Apple and your device settings.

10. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your ritual content, intentions, reflections, or usage history with advertisers, data brokers, or third-party analytics providers.

Information may be processed by Apple where necessary to provide App Store purchases, system services, backups, and other Apple-controlled functionality.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

Ritual information is stored locally on your device and is retained while the App and its local data remain on your device.

Manifest 369 does not currently provide an account-based cloud database, so there is no Manifest 369 server account containing your ritual data for us to delete.

Deleting the App generally removes its locally stored application data. Information included in an Apple-managed device backup may remain in that backup according to Apple's backup policies and your settings.

A limited technical preference used to manage the timing and integrity of in-app offer behavior may be stored in the iOS Keychain and can persist after the App is deleted. It does not contain your intentions, reflections, ritual history, or payment information.

12. Permissions

Manifest 369 currently requests notification permission only when you choose to enable reminders.

The App does not currently request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, precise location, Health data, or advertising tracking.

13. Security

We use Apple's platform security features and local application storage mechanisms to help protect information stored by the App.

No method of electronic storage or device security can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining appropriate security for your device and Apple Account.

14. Children's Privacy

Manifest 369 is not specifically directed to children, and we do not knowingly operate a service designed to collect personal information from children.

15. International Users

Manifest 369 may be available in multiple countries and regions. Because the App's ritual data is primarily stored locally on your device rather than transmitted to servers operated by us, we do not operate a central database of your ritual content.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our practices, or legal requirements change. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.

If future versions of Manifest 369 introduce features that materially change how information is collected, transmitted, or shared, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

MANIFEST 369

Privacy Policy

Your ritual is personal. We designed Manifest 369 with privacy in mind.

Last updated: October 2, 2026

1. Overview

Manifest 369 - Affirmations ("Manifest 369", "the App") is provided by EEB Consultancy LLC ("we", "us", or "our").

Manifest 369 is designed as a local-first application. We do not operate user accounts or a server that receives your manifestations, intentions, affirmations, ritual activity, reflections, or Journal entries.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To provide the App's features, Manifest 369 stores certain information locally on your device. Depending on how you use the App, this may include:

  • Your selected or custom-written intentions and affirmations.
  • Your active and previous intentions.
  • Your 369 ritual progress and completion history.
  • Morning, Afternoon, and Evening repetition counts.
  • Optional ritual reflections.
  • Optional Journey Journal entries that you choose to write.
  • Journey length, progress, and completion information.
  • Streak and ritual activity information.
  • Your selected Aura personalization preference.
  • Your selected app language and accessibility preferences.
  • Ritual reminder settings and reminder times.
  • Daily Motivation preferences, including frequency and selected categories.
  • Information necessary to remember onboarding and in-app offer states.

Custom intentions and Journal entries may contain personal or sensitive information if you choose to enter it. You control what you write in the App.

3. Morning Writing

During the Morning ritual, the App may ask you to write your intention multiple times. The text entered for these repetitions is used locally to validate the ritual and is not separately saved as a history of what you typed.

The App stores the resulting ritual progress rather than each individual Morning repetition entry.

4. Notifications and Daily Motivation

If you enable notifications, Manifest 369 may schedule local ritual reminders and optional Daily Motivation notifications on your device.

Daily Motivation uses curated content included with the App. Your selected frequency and category preferences are stored locally on your device.

Manifest 369 does not use a remote push-notification server for these notifications. Your custom intentions and Journal entries are not included in notification content.

You can change or disable these notifications at any time through the App's settings or your device's system settings.

5. Journey Journal

Manifest 369 Pro may allow you to write optional Journal entries associated with your Journey days.

Journal entries are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a server that receives or stores your Journal text, and Journal entries are not sent to analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Journal entries may be included in Apple-managed device or iCloud backups if those backup features are enabled on your device.

You may edit or clear Journal entries through the features provided within the App.

6. Widgets

Manifest 369 may provide Home Screen widgets that display limited ritual status information, progress, streak, Journey status, visual state, and curated Daily Motivation content.

Widget data is stored locally in an Apple App Group shared between Manifest 369 and its widget extension.

Your custom intention text and Journal entries are not included in the widget data.

7. Aura Personalization

Manifest 369 may allow you to select visual Aura preferences. Your selected Aura is stored locally on your device so the App can remember your personalization choice.

Aura preferences do not contain your intention text, Journal content, or other written ritual content.

8. Purchases and Subscriptions

Manifest 369 may offer Pro features through auto-renewable subscriptions and one-time in-app purchases.

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not directly collect or store your payment card information, Apple Account credentials, or App Store payment details.

The App may receive information from StoreKit necessary to determine whether a purchase or subscription is valid and whether Pro access should be available.

Eligibility for introductory offers, including free trials, is determined through Apple's StoreKit services.

9. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Manifest 369 does not include third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking SDKs.

We do not use the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA), and the App does not track your activity across apps or websites for advertising purposes.

10. Third-Party Services

The App relies on Apple-provided services where necessary for core platform functionality, including:

  • Apple App Store and StoreKit for purchases and subscriptions.
  • Apple system notifications for local reminders and Daily Motivation.
  • Apple system functionality for Home Screen widgets.
  • Apple's App Store review system when you choose to respond to a review request.

Your use of Apple services is also subject to Apple's applicable terms and privacy practices.

11. iCloud and Device Backups

Manifest 369 does not currently use CloudKit or provide its own cross-device cloud synchronization service.

Data stored locally by the App, including intentions, ritual history, and Journal entries, may be included in iCloud or computer backups of your device if you have enabled Apple's device backup features.

Those backups are managed by Apple and your device settings.

12. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your ritual content, intentions, affirmations, reflections, Journal entries, or usage history with advertisers, data brokers, or third-party analytics providers.

Information may be processed by Apple where necessary to provide App Store purchases, system services, device backups, and other Apple-controlled functionality.

13. Data Retention and Deletion

Ritual information and Journal entries are stored locally on your device and are retained while the App and its local data remain on your device, unless you edit or remove information through available App features.

Manifest 369 does not currently provide an account-based cloud database, so there is no Manifest 369 server account containing your ritual or Journal data for us to delete.

Deleting the App generally removes its locally stored application data. Information included in an Apple-managed device backup may remain in that backup according to Apple's backup policies and your settings.

A limited technical preference used to manage the timing and integrity of in-app offer behavior may be stored in the iOS Keychain and can persist after the App is deleted.

This Keychain information does not contain your intentions, affirmations, Journal entries, reflections, ritual history, or payment information.

14. Permissions

Manifest 369 requests notification permission when notification functionality requires it, such as ritual reminders or Daily Motivation.

The App does not currently request access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, precise location, Health data, or advertising tracking.

15. Security

We use Apple's platform security features and local application storage mechanisms to help protect information stored by the App.

Journal data is stored within the App's local application storage and uses iOS file-protection mechanisms.

No method of electronic storage or device security can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for maintaining appropriate security for your device and Apple Account.

16. Children's Privacy

Manifest 369 is not specifically directed to children, and we do not knowingly operate a service designed to collect personal information from children.

17. International Users

Manifest 369 may be available in multiple countries and regions. Because the App's ritual and Journal data is primarily stored locally on your device rather than transmitted to servers operated by us, we do not operate a central database containing your ritual content.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our practices, or legal requirements change. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above.

If future versions of Manifest 369 introduce features that materially change how information is collected, transmitted, stored, or shared, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
